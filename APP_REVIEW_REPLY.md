Hello,

Thank you for the review. We have addressed all four issues in build 1.0 (2):

**Guideline 5.1.1(v) – Registration required for in-app purchases**
Users can now buy Premium without creating an account. When a guest starts a purchase, the app creates an anonymous session in the background and does not collect any personal data. Users can register later if they want to sync progress across devices, and their purchase stays linked to them when they do. "Restore Purchases" also works without signing in.

**Guideline 2.1(b) – In-app purchases not submitted**
The auto-renewable subscriptions "Zentalist Premium Monthly" and "Zentalist Premium Annual" (group "Zentalist Premium") have been completed and are submitted for review together with this version, along with "Zentalist Lifetime Premium".

**Guideline 5.1.1(v) – Account deletion**
Users can delete their account in the app: Profile → Delete Account → confirm with password. This permanently deletes the account and all associated data (progress, leaderboard entry). A screen recording of the full flow, captured on a physical device, is attached [LINK / ATTACHMENT].

**Guideline 3.1.2(c) – Subscription information**
The purchase screen shows the title, length and price of each subscription. It also shows auto-renewal terms and working links to the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy. The App Description now includes a link to the Terms of Use (Apple standard EULA: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/) and to the Privacy Policy (https://zentalist.app/privacy).

Demo account (optional, purchases also work as a guest): review@zentalist.app — password in App Review Information.
